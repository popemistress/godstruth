# God the Father Course — Image Generation Prompts

Generate images as photorealistic, cinematic, and emotionally resonant. Avoid generic religious clipart or abstract symbols. Every image should feel like a real scene with natural light, texture, and depth. Use the following prompts with Recraft, DALL-E, or a similar image-generation tool.

## Production Notes

- **Style:** Photorealistic, cinematic, emotionally resonant, scene-specific.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 for lesson header images, 4:3 or 16:9 for course cover.
- **Color palette:** Warm golds, deep blues, natural earth tones, and soft divine light.
- **Avoid:** Generic clipart, flat illustrations, cartoonish figures, overly abstract symbols without human context.
- **After generation:** Upload to UploadThing and replace placeholders in `GOD/god-the-father-course.md` and `GOD/god-the-father-seed.ts`.
