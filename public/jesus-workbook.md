## Prayer of Dedication to Jesus

*Read aloud, slowly, signing your name at the bottom if you are ready to make this commitment.*

Lord Jesus Christ,

I confess that you are the eternal Son of God,
the Word made flesh,
the Lamb who takes away the sin of the world,
the risen Lord and coming King.

I have heard your question, "Who do you say that I am?"
Today, by your grace, I answer:
**You are my Lord, my Savior, and my God.**

I surrender my mind, my heart, my body, my relationships,
my work, my money, my future, and my death to you.
Cleanse me by your blood. Fill me with your Holy Spirit.
Teach me to love God with all that I am
and to love my neighbor as myself.

Make me a disciple who makes disciples,
a bearer of your kingdom,
a witness to your resurrection,
and a stranger no longer, but a child of the Father.

I pray this in your name, Jesus. Amen.

Signed: ________________________ Date: ________________
